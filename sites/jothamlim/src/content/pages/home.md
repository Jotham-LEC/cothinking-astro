## Profile

I work full time as the Head of Global Marketing at a China-based market-intelligence company, handling end-to-end B2B marketing campaigns, pipeline & infrastructure. I work part-time as a [National Board Certified Health & Wellness Coach (NBC-HWC)](https://www.credly.com/badges/e890b57b-7530-4c5a-ae3e-4fb7c31773ba) at [HealthyGamer](https://coaching.healthygamer.gg), specialising in career coaching. I also run [[CoThinking]], my web, IT and marketing practice for small businesses.

In the evenings, I join fitness classes at [GRIT Nation](https://www.instagram.com/gritnationsubang/?hl=en) or jog at a nearby park. Occasionally, I take part in dragon boat races, Hyrox and the odd Redline competition (see [[Sports]]). But most of my time goes into tinkering with Linux, building with Laravel and learning computer science.

It's difficult to explain what I do for a living because my interests and experience are so varied. 

Before Tech & Marketing, I used to work as a Journalist at [TheEdge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com) writing in-depth stories on startups, personal finance and the digital economy. I also worked as a filmmaker, photographer and editor for 10 years, covering everything from weddings to high-budget production shoots. I also completed a postgraduate degree in data science and business analytics, before ending up where I am today.

But each step of my career has built on the last, and I am extremely fortunate that my work, life and interests overlap so much.
