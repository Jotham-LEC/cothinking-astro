This notice explains how I handle personal data on cothink.ing, under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

- **Who is responsible:** I am, Jotham Lim. CoThinking is the trading name of Thinkers Collective Enterprise (SSM 202503229808 (PG0575749-P)).
- **Contact:** [jotham@cothink.ing](mailto:jotham@cothink.ing), for questions, requests, and complaints.

## What I collect

**When you email me**, I keep your email and our replies:

- your name, email address, and message, which I need to reply;
- your company, if you give it.

The buttons on this site fill in a subject naming the page you wrote from, so that I know which of my pages are useful.

**If you message me on WhatsApp**, I keep our messages and your phone number for the same purposes, and for the same periods, as an email.

**When you browse the site**, Google Analytics and PostHog record how it is used, such as the pages visited and the kind of device and browser. They set cookies to do so. I use these figures to improve the site, not to identify you.

## Why I use it

- To reply to your enquiry.
- To send you a quote.
- If we work together, to do the work and bill for it.

I don't sell your data, and I don't add you to a mailing list.

## Who else handles it

These services process data on my behalf. Some store it outside Malaysia, and each is bound by its own data protection terms.

| Service | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Hetzner | Hosts the site |
| Cloudflare | Delivers the site |
| WhatsApp (Meta) | Carries the messages you send me on WhatsApp |
| Backblaze | Stores the site's encrypted backups |
| Google and PostHog | Provide the analytics |

## How long I keep it

- **If we work together:** our correspondence and invoices for seven years, as Malaysian law requires for business records.
- **If an enquiry doesn't lead to work:** deleted after two years.

## Your rights

Email me to:

- see the data I hold about you;
- correct it;
- stop me using it.

I will reply within 21 days, as the Act requires. If you aren't satisfied with my answer, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner.
