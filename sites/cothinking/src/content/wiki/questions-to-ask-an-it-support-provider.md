Ask these 18 questions before you sign. A good provider answers in numbers, names, and documents; a weak one answers with reassurance. Get the important answers in writing. Why each topic matters is in [[How to choose an IT support provider for a small company]].

## Scope

| Question | Good answer | Bad answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. What is included in the fee, and what isn't? | Written lists of both, and how excluded work is quoted | "Everything IT, don't worry" |
| 2. How do you count users or devices? | "Per active user on the 1st; a month's notice to change" | No definition of a device |
| 3. How many on-site hours are included, and at what rate after? | A number, a rate, and the area covered | "We'll come when needed" |
| 4. What does onboarding involve and cost? | A list of what they need, a fee or "none", and a timeline | No onboarding or audit |

## Speed

| Question | Good answer | Bad answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5. What are your reply and fix times? | Both, by priority: "Critical: reply in 1 hour, fix target 4 hours" | One "response" time that is an automatic email |
| 6. What are your hours, and what happens outside them? | "8am to 6pm, Monday to Friday; urgent calls after hours at RM X an hour" | "24/7" from a team of three |
| 7. How do we log a ticket and see its status? | One address or portal, ticket numbers, a readable history | "Just WhatsApp me" |
| 8. Who will do the work? | Names, plus a second person who knows your setup | Only the salesperson |

## Security

| Question | Good answer | Bad answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9. What security will you set up in the first month? | Two-step verification, patching, endpoint protection, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC (see [[What is DMARC?]]), and a process for leavers | "We'll install antivirus" |
| 10. How do you back up, and when did you last test a restore? | What, how often, where (offline, as [NACSA](https://nacsa.gov.my/advisory9.php) advises), how long, and a date | "Google backs it up" |
| 11. What will you do if we have a data breach? | A written plan, including the 72-hour report (see [[PDPA amendments: what sales and marketing teams must change]]) | "That won't happen with us" |
| 12. How do your staff protect their access to us? | Individual logins, two-step verification, a password manager | One shared admin password |

## Ownership

| Question | Good answer | Bad answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 13. In whose name are our admin accounts, domain, and licences? | "Yours; we use our own admin accounts, which you can remove" | "We'll keep the admin login" |
| 14. Will you give us a list of every account and who has access? | Yes, kept current where you can read it | "It's all in our system" |
| 15. If you are a Microsoft partner, how will you access our tenant? | Through GDAP, which you grant ([Microsoft](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/customers/gdap-introduction)) | "We'll need Global Admin" |

## Contract and exit

| Question | Good answer | Bad answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 16. What are the minimum term and notice period? | A few months, and a stated notice period | Three years, auto-renewing, with an exit fee |
| 17. What happens to unused hours, and how is extra work billed? | A plain rule, and a rate quoted before work starts | Hours never reported |
| 18. What will you hand over when we leave? | Every credential, a handover document, their access removed, and help for the next provider, in the contract | "We've never had anyone leave" |

> [!TIP] Start with question 13
> A provider happy to put everything in your name has little to hide in the other answers.

## Using the answers

1. Score providers on how specific they were, not on how the meeting felt.
2. Check the answers against the contract: if the meeting said a 1-hour reply and the contract says "best effort", the contract wins. See [[Red flags in a website or IT quotation]].
3. Don't mark down a small local provider for less polish. Coming over the same afternoon can matter more.
