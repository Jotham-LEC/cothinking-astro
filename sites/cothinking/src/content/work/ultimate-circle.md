Ultimate Circle Sdn Bhd, one of Malaysia's largest distributors of commercial kitchen and food equipment, trades from Puchong under two brands. I have looked after its IT and website since May 2026. Within three months the company had every account back, both brands ran on one server, and quotation requests came in through a catalogue of about 400 products.

## Where it started

- The previous vendor held the domain, the hosting, and the Google administrator account, and wouldn't explain any of them.
- The company's email hadn't worked for months.
- The two brand websites sat on separate WordPress hosts with no security baseline, no backups, and no documentation.

## What I did

I recovered the domain and the Google administrator account from the old vendor, with a letter of authority ready in case the vendor refused, and documented the hosting credentials.

I moved both brands onto one managed server, set up the settings that stop the company's emails going to spam or being forged, and turned on two-step sign-in.

I built a catalogue in Laravel where customers can request a quotation, imported the specifications of about 400 products from supplier PDFs, and trained the staff to use it.

## Where it stands

I look after its helpdesk, email security, servers, and website, and each month the company pays only for the work done. Everything is registered in the company's name, and with an exit clause in the contract, Ultimate Circle can leave at any time and another firm could pick up from there.
