If customers find you every way except through your website, the site is failing at one of its three jobs: helping people find you, showing what you sell, and making it easy to get in touch. Often a fix is enough; sometimes replacing the site costs less.

## How to check

Signs you have this problem:

- Customers find you every way except the website.
- It is slow or awkward on a phone.
- Products and prices are out of date, because changing them needs a vendor.

To confirm it:

1. Open the site on your phone, on mobile data, and time how long it takes to load.
2. Contact yourself through it the way a customer would: the form, the WhatsApp button, the phone number. Check each one arrives.
3. Show the home page to someone who doesn't know the business for ten seconds. Ask what you sell, where, and how to get in touch.
4. If the site has analytics, compare visits with enquiries over the last three months.

> [!TIP] Free tools
> - [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/): how fast the site loads on a phone, and what slows it down.
> - [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console): which searches bring people to the site, and which pages Google skips.
> - [Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com/): visits and, once set up, enquiries.

## Possible causes

- It is hard to contact you. The WhatsApp button or form is missing, broken or buried.
- It is unclear what you sell, and visitors leave before they work out whether you can help.
- It is slow on a phone. Most visitors are on a phone, and many leave after a few seconds.
- Nobody can update it, so it drifts out of date.
- Nobody finds it. Then the fix lies with Google rather than the site: see [[Appear more on Google searches]].

## If you or your IT person want to do it yourselves

### Before you start

You need:

- **An admin login to the website**, and to its hosting.
- **Access to its analytics**, if there are any.
- **The three questions customers ask most**, from your sales team or your WhatsApp history.

### How to fix it

#### 1. Make contact one tap away

Put a WhatsApp link (`https://wa.me/60123456789`, your number without the `+` or leading zero), a tap-to-call number and the form in the header of every page.

#### 2. Say what you sell on the first screen

Before anyone scrolls: what you sell, for whom, where, and how to get in touch. Answer the three common questions on the page.

#### 3. Make it fast on a phone

1. Run PageSpeed Insights on the home page.
2. Shrink the large images it lists, and remove sliders, pop-ups and plugins you don't need.
3. Run it again to check.

#### 4. Check the form works

If test enquiries didn't arrive, see [[Website form not sending emails]].

#### 5. Measure enquiries

In Google Analytics, mark form submissions and WhatsApp clicks as key events, so you can see which pages and searches bring in enquiries.

#### 6. Decide: fix or replace

If nobody can edit the site, it is built on software that no longer gets updates, or the fixes add up to more than a new site, replacing it costs less. [[Websites]] has what a new site costs.

## Questions

### Can I keep my current website?

Often, yes. If fixing it costs less than replacing it, fix it.

### Who should own the website?

You. The domain should be in your company's name, and the site yours once it is paid for.
